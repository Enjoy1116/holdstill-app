# Framey video playbook — retention & engagement (read before making ANY video)

Data (Nov 7, "I Survived 100 Days" reel, 1:49): 92.6% of views came from the Reels tab.
Retention fell 100% → ~60% in the first ~8 s and → ~35% by 0:20, then stayed almost FLAT to the end.
=> The story keeps people. The OPENING loses them. Fix the first 3 seconds before anything else.

## The first 3 seconds (most important)
1. Frame 1 is already mid-action or mid-danger. No title cards, no logos, no "Day 1" setup, no slow intro line.
2. Cold open: 2–3 s flash-cut montage of the scariest/coolest moments from later in the video
   (each cut 0.4–0.6 s, white flash + hit sound on every cut), ending on a big question on screen
   ("WHO IS TEXTING HIM?"). Then start the story.
3. Big on-screen text in the first second that creates a question the viewer needs answered.
4. Sound in the first 0.1 s (bass hit / voice line). Silence at the start = scroll.
5. The first spoken line is under ~4 s, with pauses squeezed out.

## Keep them (0:03–0:20)
- A new visual every 1–2 s in the first 20 s (cut, zoom, shake, new text). Slow push-ins on every shot.
- Pay off the hook fast (a reveal / scare / twist before 0:15).
- Captions: word-by-word, big, high on screen but above the bottom UI area.

## Engagement (comments, shares, follows)
- 2–3 on-screen comment prompts INSIDE the video (not only at the end), each tied to the moment:
  "WOULD YOU OPEN THE DOOR? COMMENT YES OR NO", "WHO IS TEXTING HIM? COMMENT YOUR GUESS".
- A follow prompt around the middle: "PART 2 IS EVEN SCARIER — FOLLOW SO YOU DON'T MISS IT".
- End on a cliffhanger + a question. Post captions as a question too.
- Pin a comment: "Part 2 drops tomorrow 👀". Reply to early comments in the first hour.
- Post the next part within ~24 h while a part is getting views.

## Series rules
- Never delete/repost a video that is getting views.
- Keep story facts consistent with what's already posted.
